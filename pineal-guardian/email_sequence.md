Today, I want to share a framework I use before purchasing any supplement.

**Before buying any brain-support supplement, ask yourself:**

1. **Do I understand what the product is intended to support?**
If the claims sound too good to be true, they probably are. Look for products positioned around supporting normal function, not treating disease.

2. **Have I checked the ingredient list?**
Research the ingredients. Are they backed by any research? Are the doses transparent? Are there any interactions with medications you take?

3. **Am I taking medications or do I have a condition that warrants speaking with a healthcare professional first?**
Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider before starting any new supplement, especially if you have existing health conditions or take medications.

4. **Am I comfortable with the price and refund terms?**
Make sure you understand the pricing structure, shipping costs and return policy before purchasing.

5. **Am I expecting a miracle or willing to commit to a daily routine?**
Supplements work best as part of a broader wellness routine — not as standalone solutions.
