## Email 2
**Subject:** The mistake that's shrinking your harvest

Hey,

Most backyard gardens underperform for one simple reason: overcrowding. It feels productive to cram in extra plants, but crowded roots and poor airflow actually reduce your total yield.

The fix is spacing plants for their mature size, not their seedling size, and using vertical growing (trellises, cages, stacked containers) to multiply your usable space without taking up more ground.

**Subject:** 15 vegetables that thrive in small backyards

Hey,

Not all vegetables are worth the space in a small yard. Sprawling crops like pumpkins can eat up 50+ square feet for a handful of fruit, while compact, high-yield crops like bush beans, leafy greens, and determinate tomatoes give you far more food per square foot.

Prioritizing the right crops is one of the fastest ways to increase what you actually harvest this season.
