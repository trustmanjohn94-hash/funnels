Why You're Exhausted By 3PM (And It's Not About Sleep)

The real reason your energy crashes every afternoon comes down to tiny cellular engines most people never think about — your mitochondria.

If you're getting enough sleep but still dragging through your afternoons, the problem probably isn't rest. It's cellular. Every cell in your body has mitochondria that convert food into usable energy, and as we age, that conversion process slows down. The result: fatigue that no amount of coffee or naps can fully fix.

Your Cells Are the Real Energy Source

Mitochondria are often called the 'power plants' of your cells because they produce ATP, the molecule your body actually runs on. When mitochondrial function declines, every system that depends on energy — muscles, brain, mood — feels it first.

Why Diet Alone Often Isn't Enough

Eating well helps, but certain nutrients that directly support mitochondrial repair and ATP production are hard to get in sufficient amounts from food alone, especially as absorption efficiency naturally declines with age.

What Supports Healthy Mitochondrial Function

Nutrients like CoQ10, PQQ, and Alpha Lipoic Acid have been studied for their role in supporting the cellular machinery responsible for energy production and protecting cells from oxidative stress.

Low cellular energy doesn't just mean feeling tired — it can quietly affect focus, mobility, and overall vitality over time. Addressing it at the source, rather than masking it with stimulants, is where real, lasting change starts.
